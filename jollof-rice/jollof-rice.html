Making Jollof Rice is basically layering flavour step by step-the suace, the seasoning, the frying, then steaming the rice properly. Nigerian-style jollof especially is smoky, rich, spicy, and tomato-heavy.

What you need

Main ingredents

  • 4 cups parboiled long grain Rice
  • 5 big tomatoes
  • 3 red bell peppers(tatashe)
  • 2-3 scotgh bonnet peppers(atarodo)(less if you don`t too much heaat)
  • 2 large onions
  • Tomato paste
  • Vegetable oil
  • chicken stock oil
  • Curry powder
  • Thyme
  • Bay leaves
  • Salt
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Optional
    • Butter
    • Smoked paprika
    • Fried chicken, turkey, beef, or plaintain on the side

Step 1-Wash and Prep the Rice

Most nigerian jollof uses parboiled rice.

  1. Wash the rice until the water is less cloudy
  2. Some people parboil it first for few minutes, then drain
  3. Set it aside

This helps the rice cook evenly without becoming mashed

Step 2-Blend the Pepper Mix

Blend together:

  • Tomatoes
  • Red bell peppers
  • Scoth bonnets
  • 1 onion

Blend until smooth.

If the mix is very watery

  • boil it for a while first to reduce excess water

Too much water=soggy jollof

Step 3- Fry the Tomato Paste

This part changes everything.

  1. Heat oil in a pot
  2. Add sliced onions
  3. Fry for like 1 minute
  4. Add tomato paste

Now fry the tomato paste properly for around:

  • 5-10 miunutes

You want it darker red, not bright raw red

That frying removes the sour/raw tatse.

4-Add the Pepper Blend

Pour in the blended pepper to mix it carefuly.

Then:

  1. Stir
  2. cover partially
  3. let it fry/simmer

This can take

  • 15-25 minutes

You`ll notice:

  • oil starts rising slightly on top
  • the raw tomato smell disappears

That means the base is ready

Step 5-Season the Sauce

Add

  • Curry
  • Thyme
  • Bay leaves
  • Salt
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Chicken stock

Taste the sauce

The sauce should taste a litlle stronger/spicier than normal beacuse the rice will absorb flavour later

Step 6-Add the Rice

Pour in th the washed rice

Mix properly so all the grains are coated in sauce

Then add stock/water:

  • just enough to barely cover the rice

Too much water=softer mushy rice

Step 7-Steam the Rice

  • Reduce heat to low-medium
  • Cover tightly

A lot of people:

  • place foil/paper under the lid

That traps steam and helps the rice cook evenly.

Cook for:

  • around 25-40 minutes

Every once in a while:

  • open
  • stir gently
  • check water level

If needed:

  • add small amounts of hot water only.

Some people also add:

  • butter nea the end for richness

Final Result

Good jollof should be:

  • orange-red
  • flavourful all through
  • slightly smoky
  • grains seperate, not mashed
  • spicy but balanced

Usually served with:

  • fried plantain
  • chicken
  • turkey
  • salad
  • moi moi

Common Beginner Mistakes

  • Too much water=soggy rice
  • Not frying tomato paste enoughr=sour tatse
  • Too much tomato=overly wet jollof
  • High heat=bottom burns before rice cooks
  • Weak seasoning=bland rice

Tiny Cheat Code

If your jollof tastes flat:

  • add butter
  • a little curry
  • tiny smoked paprika
  • extra stock cube

This combo rescues a LOT of Jollof
